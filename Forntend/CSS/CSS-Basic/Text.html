Iron man

Tony Stark Iron Man is one of the most iconic superheroes from Marvel Comics and the Marvel Cinematic Universe. His real name is Tony Stark, a genius billionaire, inventor, and philanthropist. After being injured and captured, he builds a powerful suit of armor to save his life and escape. Later, he upgrades the suit with advanced technology, becoming Iron Man. Unlike many heroes, Tony doesn’t have natural superpowers—his strength lies in his intelligence, creativity, and courage. He uses his armor to fight villains, protect the world, and inspire others. Iron Man also shows that even flawed humans can rise to greatness through determination and innovation.

Solo series

Iron Man’s solo series—both in comics and films—highlight his evolution from a self-centered billionaire to a selfless hero. His solo arcs often dive deep into themes of responsibility, technology’s impact, and personal sacrifice.

Fictional character biography

Tony Stark, better known as Iron Man, is a Marvel Comics superhero created by writer Stan Lee, developed by scripter Larry Lieber, and designed by artists Don Heck and Jack Kirby. He first appeared in Tales of Suspense 1968.
